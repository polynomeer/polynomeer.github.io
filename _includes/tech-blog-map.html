{%- comment -%} Review map for /tech-reviews/: how much of what is worth reading has been read, and what was written about it. At the top, coverage (sources reviewed out of all sources) and the sources read most. Tabs pick a kind of source (Korean companies, global companies, personal blogs, conferences); another chip keeps only sources with reviews. Each reviewed source is a card with its first reviews and the rest folded; sources without a review collect in a "to read" list at the end. Parameters: `items` array of source items (each with `id`, `kind`, `name`, `blog`, `focus`, optional `links`/`note`, and either `category` or `tag`); defaults to the four _data source files concatenated `item_heading` heading tag for each source (defaults to h2) `preview` reviews shown before the fold (defaults to 3) Matching: an item with `category` collects [TechBlog, ] post reviews and [Conference, ] talk reviews; an item with `tag` (a conference) collects Conference posts carrying that tag.
