{%- comment -%} Engineering blog / conference directory and the review map (one collapsible entry per source). Reviews are matched by category: [TechBlog, ] for blog post reviews, [Conference, ] for talk reviews. An item with `tag` instead of `category` (a conference) matches Conference posts carrying that tag.
